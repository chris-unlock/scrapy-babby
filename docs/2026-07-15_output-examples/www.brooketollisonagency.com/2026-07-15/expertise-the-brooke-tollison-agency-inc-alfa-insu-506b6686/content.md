## CIC, CRM and AIC: What These Letters Mean for You

Brooke holds three of the insurance industry's most respected designations. These are not simply letters after a name — they represent hundreds of hours of advanced coursework and a demonstrated commitment to staying at the forefront of the industry.

CIC2012

### Certified Insurance Counselor

Brooke has completed advanced coursework in five key areas of insurance: commercial property, commercial casualty, personal lines, agency management, and life and health. This means she has a comprehensive understanding of the policies she recommends and can provide expert guidance across all lines of coverage.

CRM2018

### Certified Risk Manager

Brooke has demonstrated mastery in risk management principles and practice — including the analysis, control, and financing of risk. This means she can help identify potential exposures and develop strategies to mitigate them, going beyond simply selling a policy to providing true risk management counsel.

AIC2007

### Associate in Claims

Brooke has specialized knowledge in the claims process. This means she understands what happens after a loss occurs and can advocate effectively on your behalf — ensuring a smoother and more efficient experience when you need your insurance most.
